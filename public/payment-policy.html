Payment Policy

Choose the payment method that suits you when placing a Lahore grocery order.

Available methods

We currently accept JazzCash, Bank Transfer, and Cash on Delivery. JazzCash checkout is handled through JazzCash's secure hosted payment page.

Cash on Delivery

For Cash on Delivery orders, payment is collected by the rider when the order arrives. Please keep the payable amount ready.

Bank Transfer

Bank Transfer orders remain pending until the transfer is verified. Follow the account instructions shown after checkout and include your order code when contacting support.

Delivery fee

Orders above Rs. 2,000 receive free delivery. Orders below that amount have a flat Rs. 150 delivery fee.
